### Sheet 3: Recommendation  
Screenshot: [screenshot-recommendation.png]  

#### Recommended Device
**Dell Latitude 5430** *(Price: £1,099)*

**Why it fits the scenario**
- Meets all-day battery requirement (12 hours claimed)  
- Lightweight at 1.36 kg, easy for travel  
- Strong security with fingerprint reader and TPM  
- 3-year onsite warranty reduces downtime risk  
- Good balance of performance and cost  

**Risks / Limitations**
- Lower-quality webcam than Surface Pro 9  
- No touchscreen option (may limit client interaction style)  

**Mitigation**
- Provide a portable external webcam for high-quality calls  
- Use tablet mode not required for this role; mouse/keyboard preferred for data entry  

**Procurement Checklist**
- Confirm 3-year onsite warranty included  
- Order 5G module for cellular connectivity  
- Ensure BitLocker encryption enabled on delivery  
- Include protective case and docking station for office days  
- Schedule user training on CRM mobile access and security features  

**Executive Summary (136 words)**  
For our Field Sales team, the primary needs are all-day battery, portability, and reliable connectivity for CRM access and client calls. After comparing the Dell Latitude 5430 and Microsoft Surface Pro 9 against weighted criteria (battery and portability weighted highest), the Dell Latitude scored higher overall (141 vs 130). It offers superior battery life (12 hours), meets the weight requirement (1.36 kg), includes strong security features, and comes with a 3-year onsite warranty—critical for minimising field downtime. Although the Surface Pro 9 has a better display and camera, the Dell’s longer battery, lower cost, and better support align more closely with the business priorities of endurance and reliability. We recommend purchasing the Dell Latitude 5430 with the 5G module.
