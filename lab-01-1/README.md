### Improvement Recommendations

#### Quick-Win Improvement
**Implement a shared Google Calendar for equipment maintenance and cleaning schedules**

- **What changes:**  
  Move from paper checklists to a shared digital calendar with reminders.

- **Why it helps:**  
  Reduces equipment downtime, ensures health-compliance checks, saves ~2 hours per week.

- **Risks / Controls:**  
  Staff training required; backup plan needed during internet outages.

---

#### Strategic Improvement
**Integrate POS system with inventory management for real-time stock tracking**

- **What changes:**  
  Automated stock deduction per sale with predictive ordering alerts.

- **Why it helps:**  
  - Reduces food waste by ~15%  
  - Saves ~8 hours per week in manual stock counting  
  - Improves cost control and forecasting  

- **Risks / Controls:**  
  Implementation cost, staff training needs, careful data migration planning.

## DELIVERABLE 3: Reflection Document

**Filename:** `Lab01_Reflection_Sarah_Johnson.docx`

### Reflection *(248 words)*

Completing this digital audit and mapping exercise revealed how deeply digital tools are embedded in even traditional industries like hospitality. I learned that our café uses over a dozen digital systems that interconnect to support everything from taking a customer’s order to paying our suppliers. The most valuable insight was seeing how data flows between systems—customer orders in Square affect inventory in QuickBooks, which then impacts ordering and staff scheduling decisions. This interconnectedness creates efficiency but also vulnerability if one system fails.

My biggest digital skills gap is data analysis. While I can operate our POS system and generate basic reports, I lack the ability to analyse trends, visualise data, and support decision-making with evidence. This limits my contribution to strategic planning and cost control.

Of my three learning goals, spreadsheet mastery is the most important for immediate workplace performance. As I take on more managerial responsibility, analysing sales patterns, identifying waste, and optimising staffing will directly impact profitability. Developing pivot table skills will make me more effective in my role and better prepared for leadership, allowing decisions to be driven by data rather than intuition.
