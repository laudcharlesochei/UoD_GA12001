# Practical Manual  
## Lab 10: Version Control & Digital Collaboration for Shared Business Assets

## 1. Lab Overview

In modern organisations, people rarely work alone. Teams collaborate on **shared digital assets** such as documents, spreadsheets, reports, and presentations. Without proper version control, this can quickly lead to confusion, lost work, and mistakes.

In this lab, you will explore how **version control and collaboration tools** help teams work together more effectively. You will use common business-friendly platforms to see how changes are tracked, how older versions can be restored, and how accountability is improved.

This lab focuses on **practical business use**, not programming. You will use tools through their **web interfaces only**.

By the end of the lab, you should be able to confidently explain how organisations manage shared files and choose the right tools for collaboration.

---

## 2. Learning Outcomes

By completing this lab, you will be able to:

- Explain **version control** in clear, business-friendly terms
- Use basic **versioning features** in common collaboration tools
- Compare collaboration platforms for different business scenarios
- Recommend suitable tools for managing **shared digital assets** in teams
